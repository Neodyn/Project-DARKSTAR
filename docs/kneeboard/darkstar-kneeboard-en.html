ALPHA CHECK
Your own position from the bullseye. Navigation cross-check.
"Overlord, Punch 1-1, alpha check bullseye."
Alpha check, bullseye zero, one, zero, one hundred twenty two miles, twenty two thousand.

Where another human pilot on your side is. The name goes after the phrase.

Springfield 2 1, bullseye two, seven, zero, for forty miles, twenty two thousand, heading zero, niner, zero.

FROM YOU, OR FROM THE BULLSEYE

Bogey dope and threat check are measured from your aircraft — bearing and range are what you fly. Picture is from the bullseye, because it describes several groups at once.

Add the word bullseye to any of them to force the bullseye format: "Overlord, bogey dope bullseye."

ASPECT — WHERE THE NOSE POINTS

HOTcoming at you FLANKINGcrossing, roughly 45° BEAMINGacross your nose, 90° COLDrunning away

Numbers are spoken digit by digit: "zero niner zero" is 090. Due north is "three six zero", never zero.

WHEN THE ANSWER IS "STANDBY"

"…message received, standby." It heard you and is still working. The real answer follows. "…standby, working other traffic." You are over the per-pilot limit. Give it a moment; it says this once and then stays quiet until the limit clears.

NOTHING CAME BACK

  • Say "radio check" first. It answers on every frequency and needs no mission data, so it separates "bot is down" from "wrong frequency" from "it cannot see you".
  • "Loud and clear, but no radar contact on you" means your name could not be matched to your aircraft: you still get answers, but from the bullseye instead of from your jet. Fix the name — see below.
  • Did you say the wake word? Nothing without it is even listened to.
  • One call per transmission. Two questions in one go get one answer at best.
  • While the bot is speaking it does not hear you. Let it finish, then key up.

Your SRS name decides whether it can find your jet. Write it as CALLSIGN 1-1 | YourName — the part before the | has to match the callsign of the aircraft you are flying in DCS. Then BRAA calls are measured from your aircraft and the bot addresses you as "Punch 1-1" instead of reading your whole handle out.

IF IT MISHEARS YOU

The wake word is recognised offline by a small model. It is not your accent being judged — it simply hears some voices better than others.

  • Say the wake word first and clearly, then pause a beat.
  • Do not shout; a hot mic distorts more than it helps.
  • If it keeps missing your voice, tell the server admin — the spelling it hears can be added, and that fixes it for good.
